Retrieved 2026-11-12 from the public high-quality MP3 previews at
https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/233/{id}_4067126-hq.mp3

The recordings are actual LEGO bricks tapped together. Playback trims leading silence,
normalises peak amplitude and applies restrained pitch/gain variation. They are contact
recordings, not a claim of newly recorded snap-lock sounds. No synthetic rattle bed is used.

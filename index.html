SHORE TEMPLE கடற்கரைக் கோயில்

Mahabalipuram, Tamil Nadu, India

  • Rajasimha’s Pallava builders raised the Shore Temple in the early eighth century.
  • The original builders didn’t have Ctrl + Z.
  • Two Shiva shrines face opposite directions; a reclining Vishnu rests between them.
  • 1,911 pieces. Zero instruction manuals.
  • Nearby rathas were carved from single rocks. This temple was assembled as masonry.
  • Built centuries before the Rebuild button existed.
